What moves together

Every pair of weekly numbers, scored from −1 (one goes up when the other goes down) to +1 (they rise and fall together). Click a square to see the weeks behind it.

Rank correlation (Spearman) on weekly values; a pair needs at least 20 weeks where both were recorded. A link is not a cause: two things can move together because a third thing, like a season, moves both.

Any two numbers

The grid above scores a fixed set of weekly numbers. This one takes any two metrics on the site, by week or by day, from any starting year, and shows every dot behind the score.

The score is a Spearman rank correlation, as in the grid. A long stretch of life (a thru-hike, a quiet year) can make two things look linked that have nothing to do with each other week to week, so the card also gives the score with each year's average taken out, and “From” drops the early years. Stairs were not recorded before October 2018; those days are left blank, so stairs and elevation only count from then on.
